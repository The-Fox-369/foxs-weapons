# Changelog

Fox's Weapons is currently in public alpha. Each minor version introduces the next major weapon/content milestone, while the `.alpha.N` suffix identifies the alpha build for that milestone.

## 0.3.0-alpha.1 — Soul Reaper

Adds the **Soul Reaper**, a heavy scythe built around critical hits and actual life stealing.

### Added

- Soul Reaper
- Custom 3D model and texture
- Custom heavy scythe player animation
- 1450 durability
- +9 Attack Damage modifier
- -3.1 Attack Speed modifier
- 15 enchantability
- Survival crafting recipe

### Life Steal

Vanilla jump critical hits with the Soul Reaper:

- Deal an additional **½ heart** of damage to the victim
- Heal the wielder by up to **½ heart**
- Only activate on confirmed vanilla critical hits
- Only steal health when the target actually takes damage

Successful life-steal hits also create a Soul-particle trail from the victim toward the wielder.

### Existing Content

- Volcano Hammer
- Blunderbuss

## 0.2.0-alpha.1 — Blunderbuss

Adds the **Blunderbuss**, the first ranged weapon in Fox's Weapons.

### Added

- Blunderbuss
- Custom 3D model and texture
- Custom firing animation
- Iron Nugget ammunition
- 450 durability

### Left Click — Single Shot

- Fires 1 accurate pellet
- 4 damage
- Low spread
- 32-block maximum range
- 0.6-second cooldown

### Right Click — Burst Shot

- Fires 4 pellets
- Wider shotgun-style spread
- 2.5 damage per pellet
- Up to 10 total damage if all four pellets hit
- 1.2-second cooldown

### Firing Effects

- Muzzle flash
- Smoke cloud
- Pellet smoke trails
- Impact particles
- Custom firing sounds
- Shotgun-style knockback

### Existing Content

- Volcano Hammer
