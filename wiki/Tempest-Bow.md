# ⚡ Tempest Bow

> **IN DEVELOPMENT**

**Planned release:** `0.4.0-alpha.1`

**Tier:** Tier 3

The **Tempest Bow** is a storm-powered bow designed around extremely fast firing and actual vanilla lightning.

Alpha 4 has **not been assembled or uploaded yet**.

Everything on this page describes the current development version and may change before release.

---

## Draw Speed

The Tempest Bow reaches a full-strength draw after only:

**0.25 seconds**

or:

**5 game ticks**

Once the five-tick threshold is reached, the shot is treated as a fully charged vanilla bow shot.

Releasing too early does not produce a normal shot.

---

## Tempest Damage

Tempest arrows receive additional damage before normal armor mitigation.

This allows vanilla bow mechanics and enchantment effects such as Power to remain meaningful.

---

## Ability — Lightning Impact

A Tempest arrow summons **real vanilla lightning** when it impacts something.

Not fake particles.

Not cosmetic lightning.

Actual Minecraft lightning.

And the target does **not** need to be an entity.

```text
Mob        → ⚡
Player     → ⚡
Wall       → ⚡
Tree       → ⚡
Roof       → ⚡
Ground     → ⚡
You missed → still ⚡
```

The lightning appears at the arrow's actual impact location.

---

## Vanilla Lightning Behaviour

Because the bolt is real vanilla lightning, normal Minecraft lightning behaviour can occur.

That means the environment and nearby entities may react exactly as they would to a natural lightning strike.

This is intentional.

---

## Ammunition

The Tempest Bow uses normal bow ammunition.

## Enchantments

The Tempest Bow is intended to use vanilla **bow enchantments** alongside durability enchantments.

Compatibility is being tested before Alpha 4 is assembled.

```text
D L D
S B S
  C
```

- `D` — Diamond
- `L` — Lightning Rod
- `S` — String
- `B` — Bow
- `C` — Copper Ingot

## Totally Intentional Feature

A Tempest Bow held in the offhand can currently affect arrows fired while another bow is used.

This was discovered during development.

It survived.

Therefore:

**feature.**
