# 🔥 Volcano Hammer

**Release:** `0.1.0-alpha.1`

**Tier:** Tier 3

The **Volcano Hammer** is a slow, heavy volcanic melee weapon and the first weapon added to Fox's Weapons.

It is designed around heavy attacks, fire, and area control.

---

## Main Attack

The Volcano Hammer is a slow melee weapon with a heavy custom swing.

It trades attack speed for high impact and durability.

---

## Passive — Fire Resistance

While the Volcano Hammer is held in either hand, the wielder receives **Fire Resistance**.

The effect disappears shortly after the weapon is put away.

Small lava particles also drip from the held weapon.

---

## Ability — Volcano Smash

Right-click the **top face of a block** to perform Volcano Smash.

The hammer attempts to create a 3×3 ring of fire:

```text
F F F
F . F
F F F
```

`F` = fire  
`.` = safe center

The center remains open so the wielder does not immediately barbecue themselves.

Volcano Smash also produces:

- lava particles
- flame particles
- heavy smoke
- a heavy impact sound

---

## Volcano Smash Rules

- Cooldown: **6 seconds**
- Durability cost: **3**
- Maximum fire blocks: **8**
- The center is always skipped
- Replaceable vegetation may be cleared for fire placement
- Normal Minecraft fire-placement rules still apply

If no valid fire can be placed, the ability does not consume its normal cooldown or durability cost.

## Enchantments

The Volcano Hammer is intended to use a **mace-style enchantment family**.

Enchantments are still being tested during development.

---

## Recipe

```text
M D M
I C I
  B
```

- `M` — Magma Block
- `D` — Diamond
- `I` — Iron Ingot
- `C` — Magma Cream
- `B` — Blaze Rod

---

## Notes

The Volcano Hammer was the weapon that started Fox's Weapons.

It also established one of the mod's most important design principles:

**Weapons should actually do something interesting.**
